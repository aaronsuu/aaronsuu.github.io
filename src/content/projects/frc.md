As CNC Specialist on the FIRST Robotics Competition team at J.N. Burnett Secondary, I was responsible for turning CAD designs into physical robot parts.

I modeled components in Fusion 360, post-processed them into G-code, and ran the CNC mill to cut aluminum parts to tight tolerances. Getting a part wrong meant the whole robot could fail in competition, so accuracy mattered at every step from design to final cut.

The team competed against schools from the US, Canada, Brazil, Taiwan, China, and Australia, finishing 3rd place at the 2022 regional. Outside of competition, I taught 15 younger students how to set up and operate the CNC machine safely, walking them through everything from tool selection to reading technical drawings.
