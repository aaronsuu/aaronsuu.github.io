As CNC Specialist on the FIRST Robotics Competition team at J.N. Burnett Secondary, I owned the path from a CAD model to a working part on the robot.

## What I built

Custom aluminum **mechanism parts** for the robot, and the **workholding fixtures** that let us cut them repeatably on the mill.

## Process

1. **Design** the part in Fusion 360.
2. **Plan toolpaths** in Fusion's CAM workspace and post-process them to G-code.
3. **Fixture** the stock, building a custom fixture when a part needed one.
4. **Machine** the part on the CNC mill.
5. **Test fit** it on the robot, then adjust the design and recut if needed.

## Results

- **72 hours from CAD to robot:** each part went from design through initial testing to deployment on the robot within three days.
- **~$400 saved:** having an outside shop make the equivalent in steel would have cost about $400 at the time. Making it in-house cost less and avoided waiting on a vendor.
- **15 students trained:** I taught younger team members to set up and run the CNC machine safely, from tool selection to reading technical drawings.
- **3rd place at the 2022 regional,** against teams from the US, Canada, Brazil, Taiwan, China, and Australia.
