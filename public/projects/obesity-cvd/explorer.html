Method: obesity = state average of the overall (all-adults) BRFSS estimate. CVD = unweighted mean of county age-standardized, spatially smoothed death rates for adults 35–64 or 65+. The two age groups run at very different levels, so they are never mixed. New Jersey has no 2019 obesity estimate, so it is left out of that year. Correlation is across states and is an association, not proof that obesity causes heart disease.
